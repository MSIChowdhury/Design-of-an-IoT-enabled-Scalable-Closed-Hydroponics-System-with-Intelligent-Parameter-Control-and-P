# Journal shortlist and verified ranking evidence

These are three credible candidates, not acceptance predictions. The applied
audit currently fits Smart Agricultural Technology most naturally. Biosystems
Engineering remains the manuscript's existing target, but its science-based
novelty and operational-validation requirements are material hurdles. Computers
and Electronics in Agriculture is a stretch for a one-deployment descriptive
audit without a demonstrated methodological advance.

Checked 12 November 2026. Direct SCImago journal/category requests failed with
HTTP 403 or an access error. The ranking evidence below uses openly accessible
institutional and government-hosted copies of database exports, with their
actual date labels; no current authenticated JCR or SCImago access is claimed.
The follow-up checked the JIF column visually in the Romanian research-funding
agency's [JCR 2024 export, June 2025](https://uefiscdi.gov.ro/resource-865584-JCR_2024.iunie2025.pdf).
Its relevant pages establish a dated JCR Q1 entry for all three journals.

The SJR export's label is reproduced literally. Its March 2025 data date and
scores do not verify the latest 2025 performance-year ranking; do not relabel it
as the current 2026 release. It establishes the stated historical Q1 snapshot.
JCR's JIF quartile is a Clarivate/Web of Science measure; SJR is a different
Scopus-derived ranking. Neither a CiteScore percentile nor an OpenAlex-based
quartile was substituted for either. Recheck the required edition through an
institutional database before submission. No fee or subscription was incurred.

The government export contains 785 pages; only the target-journal rows and
pages 3 and 137 were inspected, not the entire export. Font-mapping warnings
occurred during extraction, so the table headers, journal identities, categories
and JIF quartile cells were checked on rendered pages. The unrelated
*Bioprocess and Biosystems Engineering* substring matches were excluded.
