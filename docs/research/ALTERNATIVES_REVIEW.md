## Why the strongest software result is still conditional

The reconciliation snapshot records durable-polling coverage of 162/10,344 opportunities without reconciliation and 10,259/10,344 with available evidence, with 57 direction-disagreeing outputs. The available-evidence row reconciles 720 episodes and adds 277,874 modeled bytes to 2,355,732 command bytes (11.80%). Those denominators repeat 3,448 historical opportunities at three phases; they are not 10,344 independent physical actions. The compact tables agree with the report on these values. All 30 local-check rows are marked passed, including six receiver crashes around recovery commit. This reviewer inspected their source tests and compact results, not the raw process journals or a fresh collection.

`Reconciler.apply` checks event/stream/digest/clock binding, terminal status and timestamps, preserves the prior conservative cooldown, commits a fresh-source barrier and never reinvokes the old command. `MockLedger` places the mock effect and completion record inside one SQLite transaction; cancellation installs a durable terminal tombstone. These are useful executable obligations. The replay supplies proof from observer-visible mock output and delivers lookup replies on the next 16 s tick. Permanently unavailable or contradictory records correctly retain the block. The observed availability improvement therefore depends on a newly available evidence service, not just better inference from unchanged receiver information.

The 100 measured rows per local profile are repeated cyclically over the historical source block. Three phases do not supply a representative network distribution. The collection uses distinct per-transaction streams/journals and a shared host clock; its low latencies do not show continuous controller throughput, independent-device synchronization, power-loss durability, mechanical dosing or general network resilience. A one-second or B=1/B=16 contract cannot be certified by a finite set of observed maxima. These limits are already disclosed in the instrumented/reconciliation assessments and must survive manuscript compression.

## Prior work that prevents a broad algorithmic novelty claim

The following are primary-source checks, with deliberately limited access claims:

- [Birrell and Nelson, *Implementing Remote Procedure Calls*, 1984, DOI 10.1145/2080.357392](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs/academic/class/15712-s19/www/papers/birrell84.pdf): indexed primary-paper section 3.2 inspected, not the full paper. Monotonic call identifiers, duplicate elimination and matching replies already underpin retry-safe RPC. Standard bounded retransmission and receipt IDs are not new contributions here.
- [Lee et al., *Implementing Linearizability at Large Scale and Low Latency*, 2015, DOI 10.1145/2815400.2815416](https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/rifl.pdf): author-hosted PDF abstract, introduction and architecture paragraphs through completion-record durability were read. RIFL explicitly couples unique operation IDs with durable completion records created atomically with effects. This directly precedes the mock-ledger completion assumption. It addresses storage operations rather than physical pumps; importing its conclusion would require proving that missing physical atomicity.
- [Burrows, *The Chubby lock service for loosely-coupled distributed systems*, OSDI 2006](https://research.google/pubs/the-chubby-lock-service-for-loosely-coupled-distributed-systems/): Google primary PDF locking/sequencer, session and failover passages inspected, not the whole paper. Recipients reject requests with obsolete lock-generation sequencers; uncertain sessions suspend work. Fencing delayed old requests and blocking while authority is uncertain are prior distributed-systems patterns, though this project's event-specific cancellation contract has different details.
- [Apache Kafka 4.0 design documentation, message delivery semantics](https://kafka.apache.org/40/design/design/#message-delivery-semantics): the official versioned semantics/transaction sections were read. It explicitly distinguishes broker processing from external-output coordination. This is corroborating engineering documentation, not a peer-reviewed comparison or a recommendation to add Kafka. The page identifies 4.0 as an older version; its cited scope is fixed to that version.

Crossref work metadata for the two ACM DOIs contained no linked update, and targeted RIFL/Chubby correction/retraction searches found no primary notice. This is a limited check, not proof of absence. An exhaustive current distributed-control literature review remains undone. Nothing above proves that the complete application-specific combination has no publishable originality; it does show that novelty must exceed recombining familiar delivery primitives on another dataset.

## What the comparisons actually isolate

The frozen delivery challenge is a stronger test than the preceding reliable-events grid because candidate and plain repetition use the same receiver, retry interval, transmission cap and no-ACK policy. Cancellation improves undesirable counts only slightly, while both pass 16/25 point criteria. Restart and negative-clock cases expose structural failures hidden by coverage/error alone. The actual change-heavy UDP probe has only nine reference events per combination and unfavorable impaired-link outcomes; a broad resilience claim would selectively ignore that evidence.

The durable timing ablations are informative because they separate durable reservations, clock/dispatch margins, recovery barriers and output wakeups. The example interval [681,687] s contains no 16 s polling tick; an earlier deadline wake can serve it. However, that output changes later cooldowns. The formula and ledger are conditional on realized history, not an optimal scheduling bound. The reported 49.94% to 66.73% ideal-link gain for B=16 is specific to the tested assumptions, and no tested setting meets all routine-profile criteria. This supports an explanation of failure and a practical scheduler correction rather than a claim that half the commands must always be lost under durability.

For liveness, a per-sensor learned run threshold is a transparent gate whose false-refusal costs are large on quantized backgrounds. The follow-up includes the needed direct bound/repeat comparators and corrects current-fault versus held-source attribution. It still does not isolate sensor malfunction from legitimate stability or show superiority at matched availability. The original supervised/one-class and exhaustive results should not be promoted over the current negative result without resolving the historical design limits below.

## Additional historical-path limitations identified in source

- `src/aasvr/exhaustive.py:102`: tune/validation/test trial descriptors sample starts from the same complete frame with different seeds. They need not use disjoint source backgrounds. Different fault seeds are not an independent holdout. `build_sweep_grid` also derives setting IDs with Python `hash` over values containing strings, so IDs can vary with interpreter hash randomization. Existing tests check shape/injection, not cross-process identifier stability or temporal separation. These findings concern the historical exhaustive path, not the current blocked LiveGate windows.
- `src/aasvr/loaders.py:33`: generic measurements are timestamp-sorted by `canonicalize_measurements`, then native fault labels are assigned from raw input order. An unsorted external CSV can consequently misalign values and labels. Existing loader tests use sorted input. Treat unverified generic external scores cautiously until this case is tested/fixed and affected runs identified.
- `src/aasvr/baselines.py:58`: detector history capacity depends on `window`, whereas GLR later asks for two `glr_window` segments. The actual window is capped by available history. PCA/recursive-PCA paths are explicitly univariate proxies. These implemented baselines do not establish superiority over fully specified multivariate process models or all optimized sequential detectors.
- At initial inspection, `configs/datasets/hydro_exp1.yaml` retained private/first-experiment wording and the absent `hydro_exp2` file was labeled a target holdout. The subsequent diff was reviewed: both are now corrected to public experiment-2 provenance and an unavailable placeholder. Compatibility names do not create a separate validation population.
- `src/aasvr/evaluation.py` uses historical sample-based notions of false/missed actuation, including cooldown samples. The later `reanalysis.py` opportunity-matching definitions are different. Do not merge numerical results across those definitions or relabel old tables as the current outcome.

These issues should be logged with their affected scope before any historical result is reused. They are not a reason to mutate frozen protocols or replace null outcomes with a favorable rerun.

## Completion standard and remaining blockers

The active audit now supports a material **scoped** result: provenance-aware replay and simple direction/range/persistence checks substantially change reconstructed commands on this released log. Exact baseline reproduction and the declared timing/attribution sensitivity make that case more defensible. They also falsify the stronger zero-pH prediction: 54 of 108 cells have one to three flagged commands. The reduced claim must remain tied to the original setting and the reported sensitivity envelope.

The user’s stronger completion standard is still not met. A distinctly new algorithmic contribution suitable for a strong Q1 novelty claim has not been demonstrated; the literature already contains hydroponic fault diagnosis, sensor/actuator consistency checks, and standard durable delivery/reconciliation patterns. The current contribution is a transparent retrospective audit with a negative method comparison. There is no independent deployment replication, continuous reference truth, recorded historical commands, physical recovery contract, or causal crop/control benefit. Passing software, lint and PDF checks establishes package integrity, not these missing scientific claims. Journal fit and novelty remain editorial/scientific judgments; this review cannot certify publication readiness from the current evidence.

The minimum decisive next evidence depends on the selected claim. For a stronger hydroponic fault-to-command contribution, predeclare a new independent run with reference sensing and retained commands/actuator effects, comparing the simplest applicable gates at matched useful-action availability. For the software recovery direction, test the same evidence contract against a strong durable/idempotent comparator with independent physical or instrumented effect evidence. Neither more overlapping injections nor longer cyclic replay closes those gaps. Do not reinterpret a rejected hypothesis as confirmed merely by narrowing its wording after inspection; preserve both the rejection and the narrower descriptive result.
