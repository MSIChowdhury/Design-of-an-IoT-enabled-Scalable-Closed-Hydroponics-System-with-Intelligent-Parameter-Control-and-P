## What the complete texts change

**Broad priority claims about connecting fault detection to agricultural actuation would be untenable. The present narrower descriptive question remains distinguishable, but its Q1 novelty is not established by that distinction alone.** The current introduction already recognizes older diagnosis and different endpoints; the full texts justify making the overlap more explicit.

Ferentinos, Albright and Selman (2003), DOI **10.1016/S0168-1699(03)00012-7**, used measured environmental histories and acid/oxygen control signals in neural fault detection for deep-trough lettuce. Physical pump stoppages and altered sensor signals tested different fault mechanisms. The general detector had six test cases; biological experiments used plant covering. Some fault outputs returned toward normal; smoothing reduced alarms at a delay cost. This is substantial prior agricultural command-aware diagnosis, with limited test populations, rather than an independent benchmark for our authorization endpoint. All 21 PDF pages were read as extracted text; selected result figures/tables were also viewed. The control-input passage is **§2.2, printed p. 69 (PDF p. 5), paragraphs 1–2**, corresponding to local extracted lines 194–214. [Author-hosted full text](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/selman/papers/pdf/03.comp-elec-agri.neural.pdf).

SensorTalk (2019), DOI **10.3390/s19214788**, is closer than an alert-only characterization suggests. Its actuator test monitors response after an instruction; calibration routes standard-sensor readings to actuators, then uses a fitted mapping for the device under test. Its reported sub-0.7% false-alarm claim is conditional on the response-time model and selected timeout, rather than a universal measured reliability guarantee. Calibration tables contain 12 pairs per illustrated variable. These mechanisms predate separating diagnosis, fallback measurement and control use. They do not perform the present held-source attribution/availability audit. The scientific text, Appendix A and references were read, with both calibration tables checked against publisher-deposited XML; appendix derivations were not independently proved. Exact locators are **§4.2, Figure 10 discussion** (instruction/response, publisher browser lines 274–281), and **§5, Figure 14 discussion**, paragraphs beginning with both-device connection and calibration completion (reference routing/mapping, browser lines 346–351). Browser line numbers are access-session locators; section/figure locators are stable. [Publisher full text](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/19/21/4788).
