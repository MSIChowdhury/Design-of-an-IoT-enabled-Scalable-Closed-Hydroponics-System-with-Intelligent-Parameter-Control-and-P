\subsection{Exploratory post-hoc variant}
After the locked run, an exploratory variant floored the forecast scale at the sensor's reading resolution. All 66 prior evaluated rows reproduced exactly, and the variant's selected settings and results matched locked LiveGate at both feasible targets. The conformal threshold is calibrated on scores divided by the same scale, so rescaling moves the scores and threshold together. This variant did not address the forecast component's observed authorization tradeoff; it is retained as a disclosed null result.
