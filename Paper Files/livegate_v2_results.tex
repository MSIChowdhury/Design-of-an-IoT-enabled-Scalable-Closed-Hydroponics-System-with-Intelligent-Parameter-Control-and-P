\subsection{Exploratory Post-hoc Variant}
After the locked run, we noticed that the forecast scale had degenerated on the coarsely quantized temperature channels. This led us to test an exploratory variant that floors the forecast scale at the reading resolution of each sensor. All 66 prior evaluated rows reproduced exactly, and the selected settings and results of the variant matched locked LiveGate at both feasible targets. Upon investigation, we learned that the conformal threshold is calibrated on scores divided by the same scale, so rescaling moves the scores and the threshold together. As such, the variant did not change the authorization tradeoff of the forecast component, and we retain it as a disclosed null result.
