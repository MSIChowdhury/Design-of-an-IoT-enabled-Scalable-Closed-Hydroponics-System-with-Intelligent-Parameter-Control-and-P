# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript "Actuation-Aware Sensor Validation and Rectification for Fault-Resilient Closed-Loop Measurement and Control" as a Research Article for ISA Transactions.

The manuscript proposes AASVR, a general method for coupling sensor validation, measurement rectification, and actuation authorization in closed-loop systems. Unlike conventional filters that evaluate measurements independently of downstream control decisions, AASVR explicitly considers whether a measurement is trustworthy enough to trigger physical actuation.

Hydroponic controlled-environment agriculture is used as the primary real deployment testbed, while external process-control datasets are used to evaluate cross-domain robustness. The contribution is the actuation-aware data-rectification method and evaluation protocol, not the web dashboard, IoT implementation, or crop-yield comparison.
